Key features of Himal Smart Home

Local control

Control your smart home devices over your home Wi-Fi network without needing internet. Works reliably across Nepal including Kathmandu, Pokhara and Biratnagar.

Online MQTT control

Access your devices remotely when online MQTT is configured. Control your home from anywhere in Nepal or abroad.

Timers & schedules

Set timers and schedules for lights, fans and sockets. Automate your daily routine with the best smart home device Nepal offers.

IR remote learning

Learn and replay IR commands for compatible appliances like TVs, ACs and set-top boxes. Compatibility depends on the appliance.

OTA firmware updates

Over-the-air update support keeps your smart home controller up to date with the latest firmware improvements.

4 smart outputs

Four relay outputs to control lights, fans, sockets and extra appliances. Confirm ratings for your exact model before installation.
