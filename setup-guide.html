Himal Smart Home setup & pairing guide
for Nepal homes.

Step-by-step guide to setting up your Himal Smart Home controller — Wi-Fi hotspot, app pairing, connection modes and reset. Best smart home device in Nepal, serving Kathmandu, Pokhara, Biratnagar and all Nepal.

Before you begin

Make sure your Himal Smart Home controller has been installed by a qualified electrician. Mains wiring can be dangerous — always switch off main power before working on wiring.

Setup steps

  1. Check the model manual: Identify your controller model and follow the included manual. Different models may have different output labels and setup steps.
  2. Power on: After the electrician has completed and checked the installation, power the controller on as described in its manual.
  3. Connect to setup Wi-Fi: Use the app or browser flow supplied for your model to connect to the controller's setup hotspot (typically 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi).
  4. Pair the app: Complete the pairing process in the app. Connection status should appear once paired.
  5. Choose control mode: Set up local control for your home network, or configure MQTT online control for remote access from anywhere.
  6. Test outputs: Turn each channel on and off to confirm your lights, fans and sockets respond correctly.

Common questions

Does setup work without internet?
Local control over your home network may work without internet. Remote online access requires the configured MQTT connection.
What Wi-Fi band is supported?
The manual mentions 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi. Make sure your router broadcasts a 2.4 GHz network during setup.
How do I reset the controller?
Many models use RESET held for 5 seconds to pair and 10 seconds for reset. Confirm timing in your model's manual.
