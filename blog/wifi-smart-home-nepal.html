Wi-Fi smart home in Nepal
Set up smart home on your Wi-Fi.

Set up a Wi-Fi smart home in Nepal with Himal Smart Home. Works with 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi routers common across Nepal. Serving Kathmandu, Pokhara, Biratnagar, Itahari, Dharan, Birgunj and all Nepal.

Wi-Fi requirements for smart home in Nepal

Most smart home devices in Nepal, including Himal Smart Home, use 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi. Before setting up, make sure:

  • Your router broadcasts a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network
  • Your phone connects to the same 2.4 GHz network during setup
  • Your Wi-Fi signal reaches the switchboard location
  • You have the Wi-Fi password ready for pairing

How to set up Wi-Fi smart home with Himal

  1. Have a qualified electrician install the Himal Smart Home controller.
  2. Power on the controller after installation is checked.
  3. Connect your phone to the controller's setup Wi-Fi hotspot.
  4. Use the app to pair the controller with your home 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi.
  5. Test each output (light, fan, socket, extra) from the app.

Local vs online control

Himal Smart Home offers two control modes:

  • Local control: Control devices over your home Wi-Fi without internet. Works even when your internet is down.
  • Online control (MQTT): Control devices remotely from anywhere when online access is configured.
