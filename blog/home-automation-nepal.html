Home automation in Nepal
Automate your Nepali home.

Home automation in Nepal is now easier than ever. With Himal Smart Home, you can automate lights, fans, sockets and appliances from your phone. Serving Kathmandu, Pokhara, Biratnagar, Itahari, Dharan, Birgunj and all Nepal.

What is home automation?

Home automation lets you control household devices automatically or remotely. In Nepal, this means scheduling lights to turn on at sunset, turning off fans when you leave home, and controlling appliances from your phone — even when you're away.

Benefits of home automation for Nepali homes

  • Energy savings: Schedule devices to turn off when not needed, reducing electricity bills.
  • Convenience: Control all your devices from one app — no more walking to every switch.
  • Security: Turn lights on and off while away to make your home look occupied.
  • Local control: Himal Smart Home works on your home network without internet.
  • Made for Nepal: Designed for Nepal's 230V power system and 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi.

What you can automate with Himal Smart Home

The Himal Smart Home controller has 4 relay outputs. You can automate:

  • Living room lights
  • Bedroom fans
  • Kitchen sockets
  • Extra appliances (water pump, geyser, etc.)
  • IR-controlled devices (TV, AC, set-top box)

How to start home automation in Nepal

  1. Contact Himal Product and Services in Morang, Nepal.
  2. Have a qualified electrician assess your wiring.
  3. Install the Himal Smart Home controller.
  4. Set up the app and pair your devices.
  5. Create schedules and timers for your routine.
