What is a smart home device?

A smart home device connects to your home Wi-Fi and lets you control lights, fans, sockets and appliances from your phone. In Nepal, popular smart home devices include smart home controllers, smart switches, smart bulbs and smart plugs.

Frequently asked questions

Do smart home devices work without internet in Nepal?
Local control over your home Wi-Fi can work without internet. Remote access needs an internet connection.
Which Wi-Fi band do smart home devices use in Nepal?
Most smart home devices, including Himal Smart Home, use 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi which is common across Nepal.
Can I install a smart home device myself?
Mains wiring should be done by a qualified electrician. Device setup via app can be done by the user.
