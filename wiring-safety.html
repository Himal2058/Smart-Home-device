Smart home wiring & safety guide
for Himal Smart Home in Nepal.

Important safety guidance for installing the Himal Smart Home controller. Always use a qualified electrician for mains wiring. Best smart home device in Nepal — serving Kathmandu, Pokhara, Biratnagar and across Nepal.

Safety first

  • Turn off main power before any wiring work. Verify with a tester that power is off.
  • Use a qualified electrician for all mains wiring, load inspection and protection checks.
  • Confirm the input voltage printed on your unit (typically 90–250 V AC, 50/60 Hz) before connecting.
  • Check load ratings — do not exceed the relay output rating for any connected light, fan or socket.
  • Keep the controller away from water and moisture — install in a dry, ventilated enclosure.
  • Double-check all connections before powering on the system.

Installation checklist

  1. Hire a qualified electrician to assess your existing wiring.
  2. Confirm neutral wire availability at the switchboard.
  3. Verify load type and rating for each appliance (light, fan, socket).
  4. Install the controller in a suitable enclosure with adequate space.
  5. Connect wires according to your model's manual and terminal labels.
  6. Power on and test each output with the electrician present.

Model-specific notes

Different Himal Smart Home models may use different output labels (such as Light, Fan, Socket, Extra or OUT1–OUT4). Always use the label and manual supplied with your exact unit. Do not assume one model's wiring applies to another.

Warning: Incorrect wiring can cause electric shock, fire or damage to appliances. Always consult a qualified electrician in your area — Kathmandu, Pokhara, Biratnagar, Itahari, Dharan, Birgunj or anywhere in Nepal.
