About Himal Product and Services
Makers of the best smart home device in Nepal.

Himal Product and Services is a Nepali company based in Sundarharaicha-4, Morang. We develop the Himal Smart Home controller — a locally made smart home device for homes across Kathmandu, Pokhara, Biratnagar, Itahari, Dharan, Birgunj and all Nepal.

Our story

Himal Product and Services was founded with the mission to make smart home technology accessible, affordable and reliable for Nepali households. Based in Morang, we design and support the Himal Smart Home controller with local customers in mind.
