Smart home device FAQ — Nepal

Answers about Himal Smart Home controller price, internet requirements, Wi-Fi, pairing, output channels, IR learning and safe installation in Nepal. Best smart home device in Nepal.

What is the Himal Smart Home controller?

It is a locally developed home controller from Himal Product and Services in Sundarharaicha, Morang. Product materials show four relay outputs for controlling supported electrical loads using app and manual controls, depending on the model.

Can a Himal smart switch work without internet?

Product development notes describe local control over the home network and MQTT online control. Local use may be available without internet when the phone and controller are connected to the same local network. Check the current model and app behavior; remote online access needs the configured online connection.

How many appliances can the controller control?

Four relay outputs are shown in the supplied materials. The number of channels does not by itself confirm which loads are suitable: output ratings, appliance type and wiring must be checked against the exact model by a qualified electrician.

Does the controller support timers and schedules?

Timers and schedules are listed in product development notes. Available options can depend on the app and firmware version. Ask the supplier to confirm the current software before purchase.

Can I use IR remote learning with any appliance?

No universal compatibility is implied. IR learning is described for compatible appliances, but some remotes use unsupported protocols or non-infrared signals. Confirm your appliance model with the supplier.

How do I connect the controller to Wi-Fi?

Follow the app and model-specific manual. The development notes mention a device setup hotspot; the manual image mentions 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi. See the pairing and setup guide and verify the steps for your unit.

How do I pair or reset the controller?

The manual artwork supplied with the product describes holding RESET for 5 seconds to pair and 10 seconds for reset. Confirm the button timing in the manual for your exact model. A factory reset may erase saved setup information.

Can the controller be installed in an existing home?

That depends on the existing wiring, available neutral, protection, enclosure space and the connected load. Ask a qualified electrician to assess the site and confirm compatibility before buying or wiring.

What is the price of the Himal Smart Home device in Nepal?

Price and availability can change, and no fixed price is published on this page. Contact Himal Product and Services for the current price, stock, warranty, installation options and delivery terms.

Does Himal deliver throughout Nepal?

Contact the supplier to confirm current delivery locations, shipping cost and expected delivery time. Coverage includes Kathmandu, Pokhara, Biratnagar, Itahari, Dharan, Birgunj and all Nepal.

Who should install a smart home controller?

A qualified electrician should handle mains wiring, inspect the load and protection, and verify operation before the system is powered. See the wiring and safety guide.
