Wiring Safety

Ensure a safe installation for your smart home.

Guidelines

  • Turn off power at the breaker before any work.
  • Use UL‑listed cables and connectors.
  • Do not exceed the recommended current load.
  • Secure all wiring inside conduit where required.
  • Consult a licensed electrician for complex setups.
